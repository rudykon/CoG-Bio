The research

Abstract

Accurate ultrasound biometry underpins fetal growth assessment and cardiac measurement. It requires landmarks that are localized precisely. These landmarks must also form consistent measurement geometry across images. Vision foundation models provide strong general-purpose features, but turning them into reliable measurement tools requires adapting them to the structured outputs of each clinical task. In this paper, we present CoG-Bio, which adapts a frozen DINOv3 foundation encoder into a single multi-task system for nine ultrasound biometry tasks spanning fetal, cardiac and vascular anatomy. Two low-rank adaptations of this encoder, a merge-refined predictor (MRP) and an independently adapted predictor (IAP), are trained with distinct histories and supervision weights, so that they produce complementary landmark configurations. For each image, the view disagreement of each predictor under fixed flip and crop policies sets a bounded fusion weight shared by all landmarks. Conditional shape repair corrects outlying endpoints, and caliper projection enforces the geometry defined by the annotation schema. On 2,384 development images, consistency weighting reduces task-macro mean radial error (MRE) from 26.40 to 26.16 pixels relative to equal averaging, and geometric correction further reduces it to 25.69 pixels. On the 1,281-image released nine-task benchmark, which played no role in selecting candidate configurations, the final system achieves 23.67 pixels, compared with 24.20 for MRP and 24.07 for equal averaging. Ablations and qualitative analyses isolate the contribution of each stage and identify where stable, geometrically valid predictions still fail, marking where human review remains necessary.

01

Learn complementary predictors

A frozen DINOv3 encoder supports two low-rank adaptations. MRP refines a merged adapter; IAP is adapted independently with different supervision weights. Their training histories produce distinct landmark configurations.

Shared architecture · Different training
02

Fuse by view consistency

Fixed flip and crop views measure each predictor’s disagreement. One bounded fusion weight is shared by all landmarks in an image, preserving the relation between corresponding caliper vectors.

Image-level weight · All endpoints
03

Enforce measurement geometry

Conditional shape repair corrects extreme cardiac configurations. HC and FA caliper projection enforces the geometric relations specified by the annotation schema.
